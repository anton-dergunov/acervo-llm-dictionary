Loops

Your words over music, with the answer held back until it is spoken

Pick the words; a loop says each one, leaves a beat to recall it, then gives the translation, over music made for it. Made by LexiBeat, a separate generator.
Kinds of loop
  • Classic drill. Each word three times with its translation, and a pause to recall it.
  • Alternating. The same, straight through.
  • Radio lesson. An intro, each word with an example and sometimes a remark, a quiz halfway, then every word again.
  • Story. A short story told in pieces between the words.
  • And two more: say it back, and review.
On the screenA radio lesson. “ir de cabeza” and its translation have been said; its example is being said now, and the answer is still a bar.
How it is made
The wordsYou choose them: a topic, a selection from the list or the map, or a number of words.
The voiceExpressive on purpose, so a word sticks. Said three times, it is three recordings, each with its own direction from a language model.
The musicGenerated new for every loop, in a style you pick or a surprise: a rhythmic background made to sit under speech. Keep one you like and reuse it.
Chosen by ear, with scores687 clips scored 1 to 5 on a tablet: rhythms, examples, stories, commentary, voices. Stories and commentary scored highest (4.3, 4.6). Asked to speak “fast”, a voice is about 1.3× quicker; a fancier direction is as often slower, so speed is set by stretching the audio.lexibeat · experiments/programme_blocks
A translation is never drawn before it has been spoken, and dragging backwards puts it away again.
