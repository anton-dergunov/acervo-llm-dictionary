How a picture is made
1The meanings of the word, each with its definition and an example sentence.
2A language model writes a brief for each: one concrete scene that shows this meaning and not the others, exaggerated so it sticks. It picks one of 23 styles for the idea; you can choose another and redraw.
3An image model draws each brief.
Tuned by hand, over eight roundsEach round, the author looked at every picture, kept or rejected it and wrote why; the brief prompt was changed and the round repeated: 2,285 pictures. Round one rejected 7 of 14; the last, 0 of 50. Among the rules that came out of it:
  • show the meaning itself, never hint at it
  • exaggerate by adding to the subject
  • keep the cast small, one situation
  • what separates two meanings must be in the frame
