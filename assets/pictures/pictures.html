How a picture is made
1The meanings of the word, each with its definition and an example sentence.
2A language model writes a brief for each: one concrete scene that shows this meaning and not the others, exaggerated so it sticks, in one of 23 styles it picks for the idea.
3An image model draws each brief.
MeasuredThe prompt that writes briefs was tuned by looking at its pictures: eight rounds, 2,285 pictures. In the first round 7 of 14 were rejected; in the last, 0 of 50. Twenty kinds of failure were fixed in the prompt. The image model never changed.experiments/sense-images
An entry also carries, for each meaning, a definition in the language you are learning, translations and examples; the picture is drawn from them.
