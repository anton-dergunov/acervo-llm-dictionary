Every meaning you hold, laid out by what it means

Words that mean similar things sit together, in regions named for you. Find a word, see its neighbours, open its entry, or pick words here for a loop or a story.
The whole map: 1,457 meanings in named regions
How it is made
1Embed each meaning, not each word, with a multilingual encoder (multilingual-e5-small).
2Lay out in two dimensions with UMAP.
3Group into about 8 regions and 30 neighbourhoods; a language model names them.
You learn where things are on a map, so adding a word should not move the rest. Measured: with a fixed seed it still moved; starting from the last layout and aligning to it cut the median move after one edit from 140 to 37 units (experiments/meaning-space).
