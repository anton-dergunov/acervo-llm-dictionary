Design choices were measured

Each prompt, model and pipeline step was tested on real words before it was kept, and some were dropped. Six of the 13 experiments:
Question
Measured
Decision
What must the brief say for a picture to be kept?experiments/sense-images
Eight review rounds over 2,285 pictures. Rejects went from 7 in 14 to 0 in 50 with the image model unchanged.
Brief once for the word, draw each sense; rotate the style table
Does the clip selector translate the whole passage?experiments/clip-translation
With the demand in the prompt, badly cut-off translations fell from 8 to 0 and full coverage rose from 81% to 91% (p = 0.004).
Prompt shipped; a check in code waits for data in more scripts
Do earlier pictures as references keep a story's characters?experiments/story-picture-reference
Three blind runs. 8 to 0 in drawn styles and 0 to 4 in the photoreal one, then 9 to 0 with 3 ties once the prompt put the picture first.
On for every style, anchored to a character's first picture
Which OCR and sentence splitter for a tapped word?experiments/photo-capture
The tap found the word 98% of the time with Cloud Vision, 68% with a local engine. SaT found the sentence for 98%, rules for 69 to 74%.
Cloud Vision for geometry, SaT for sentences, a fast model for the tap
Do two more fields in the prompt thin the entry?experiments/compose-lesson-line
No: ten measures moved less than run-to-run noise over 315 calls. The blind pairwise read called a difference on 50% of identical-arm controls.
Fields shipped, prompt not split; pairwise judging not relied on
Does the meaning map stay put when words are added?experiments/meaning-space
Not with a fixed seed: it came back mirrored. A warm start and an alignment cut one edit's median move from 140 to 37 units.
Warm start and Procrustes alignment on every rebuild
