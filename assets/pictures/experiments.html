Experiments

Design choices were measured

Prompts, models and pipeline steps were tested on real words before they were kept. Eight experiments, here and in the two projects Acervo is built on:
Question
Measured
Decision
Which style does the model pick from a list?Pictures · experiments/sense-images
Offered in file order it kept picking the first row. After the list was rotated, one round used 20 of the 23 styles and skipped three whose descriptions did not say what they are for.
Rotate the list for each word; every style says what it suits
Is the whole passage translated?Clips · experiments/clip-translation
Old prompt against a rewritten one, 343 calls on 3 models: badly cut-off translations fell from 8 to 0, fully covered ones rose from 81% to 91% (p = 0.004).
The rewritten prompt is the one in use
Do earlier pictures as references keep a character the same?Stories · experiments/story-picture-reference
Picture sets drawn with and without references, compared blind, side by side: with references preferred in 9 of 12, 3 ties, none against.
References are used for every picture style
Do two more fields in the prompt make the rest of the entry thinner?Entries · experiments/compose-lesson-line
No. Ten measures of an entry's substance each moved less than their own run-to-run noise, over 315 calls.
The fields were added to the one prompt, with no second call
Does the map stay put when a word is added?Map · experiments/meaning-space
You learn where things are, so it should. With a fixed seed it came back mirrored; starting from the last layout and aligning to it cut the median move from 140 to 37 units.
Every rebuild starts from the previous map and is aligned to it
Search by the form typed, or by the dictionary form?Spoken Usage Retrieval · experiments/morphological-retrieval
By dictionary form, the verb estar goes from 24 found uses to 499.
Search by dictionary form by default
Do captions need re-aligning to the audio?Spoken Usage Retrieval · experiments/forced-alignment
On captions written by people, alignment cut word timing error from 333 ms to 59 ms. Automatic captions were already as close as the aligners agree with each other.
Align only captions written by people
How should a voice be helped to say a hard word?LexiBeat · experiments/programme_blocks
687 clips scored by ear. Said syllable by syllable, words came out wrong half the time; the whole word said slowly was right for 14 of 15.
The whole word, slowly; syllable by syllable was dropped
