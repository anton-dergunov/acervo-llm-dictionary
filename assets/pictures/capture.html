Paste the sentence; read the entry before it is saved

The word is picked out of the sentence you met, the entry is written, and nothing is stored until you press Save.
1 · Paste what you read
Paste a word or a whole sentence. The word or phrase is worked out for you; name it only to settle a doubt. The sentence is kept as your own example.
→
2 · Read the generated entry
Definition, translations, pronunciation and notes, written in two model calls. Pictures, clips and recordings are added in the background after you save.
→
3 · Ask for changes
Talk to the draft: add or drop an example, question a translation. Each answer is a proposed edit to review.
The same pipeline takes a photographed page, a file of notes, and a word tapped in a story. Measured: two fields added to the compose prompt did not thin the entry over 315 calls (experiments/compose-lesson-line).
