# Round 2: the owner's notes

These are the owner's spoken notes from judging round 2 on the tablet, edited for reading. The opinions
are the owner's. Each blind letter is followed by the picture it was, from [`key.json`](key.json): model
(NB2 = Nano Banana 2, Pro = Nano Banana Pro), way and take. Characters are numbered as the page
numbered them.

## Overall

- **The commonest failure is over-integration.** The model works the character into the picture too
  hard and draws something extra onto it.
  - Branches grow out of 木's strokes.
  - Parts of the character turn into parts of the picture.
  - Ground climbs up over the trunk.

  No text and no numbers appeared this time.
- **The character must be drawn on top.** In some of the best ideas the picture covers the strokes. A
  light overlap is fine. Covering them heavily is not.
- **Proportions.** The owner asked whether they matter, and how exact a picture must be to the reference.
  - In 林 the right tree is taller than the left.
  - In 舟 one picture made the character much wider.
  - The answer is in the README.
- **Stroke direction.** Without the reference a slanting stroke sometimes turned straight. 舟 A's
  middle dot ran straight down.
- **People need clothes and a face.** 子 and 立 were drawn without eyes, without a face, or without
  clothes.
- **Aligning parts with strokes** is what the owner chooses by, beyond the two questions, along with
  how the picture looks.
- **The owner's guesses** about which pictures were drawn over the reference, checked against the key:
  26 of 31 were right.
  - Guesses made from a stroke error were right: 爪 8 of 8, 旦 8 of 8, 女 G and H, 伞 F.
  - Guesses made from how well a picture fits the character were mixed.
    - 禾 F and G are text pictures.
    - 炎 D and F are text pictures.
    - 舟 A is an edit picture.
- **A later pass.** Once every character has been drawn, the owner will go over them again and redraw
  those they are not fully happy with, keeping the current star. This round asks for only 月 to be
  redrawn.
